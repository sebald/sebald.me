# Spacing

Every space in the kit is named for **what it is for**, never by a
number. `gap-related` says "a thing and its own parts", `p-window` says
"inside a window". Whoever builds a screen picks by meaning, and two
windows built on different days come out the same.

The lengths are in stage pixels (`--bui-px`, never finer than
`--bui-min-px`). They sit on two ladders, each step double the last:

- **1, 2, 4, 8 lays things out:** every gap and every padding.
- **3, 6, 12 is the cursor's** (`focus.mdx`), and nothing else's.

So a 3 or a 6 outside the cursor's room is a mistake, and two neighbouring
choices are never a pixel apart.

## The roles

| Role            | Step | For                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gap**         |      | between neighbours, by how related they are                                                                                                                 |
| `joined`        | 1    | Repeats of one thing: a menu's rows, a meter's cells (compact)                                                                                              |
| `related`       | 2    | A thing and its own parts: a label and its control, a control and its description, a title and its blurb, a mark and its text, the lines of a ledger        |
| `regular`       | 4    | Separate items of one group: a form's fields, a menu's groups, a name and its value, the content and the buttons under it, a title and its window's content |
| `section`       | 8    | Regions: the stage's parts, windows side by side                                                                                                            |
| **Inset**       |      | the padding inside, by what holds it                                                                                                                        |
| `control`       | 2    | Inside a control, on its block axis: a menu row, a corner button                                                                                            |
| `field`         | 4    | Inside a text field, above and below its text: a line to write on, a thumb's height                                                                         |
| `control-x`     | 4    | Inside a control, on its inline axis: a button's label, a tab's                                                                                             |
| `window`        | 4    | Inside a window or a speech bubble                                                                                                                          |
| `edge`          | 8    | From the stage's or a screen's edge, and round a dialog                                                                                                     |
| **Cursor**      |      | the cursor's room, for nothing else                                                                                                                         |
| `cursor`        | 3    | The cursor to what it points at                                                                                                                             |
| `cursor-clear`  | 6    | Nothing else nearer the cursor on its side; the room a bar button keeps for it                                                                              |
| `cursor-apart`  | 12   | Between focusable neighbours (`Actions`)                                                                                                                    |
| `cursor-column` | 8    | The column left of a row or the first button, which the cursor sits in                                                                                      |

## Which one?

1. **Is it the cursor's room?** Then it is a `cursor-*`, whatever else is
   near.
2. **Is it inside something** (between a box's edge and what it holds)?
   Then it is an inset: `control`/`control-x` in a control (`field` above
   and below a text field's text), `window` in a
   window, `edge` at the stage or a screen.
3. **Otherwise it is a gap.** Ask how related the two are: the same thing
   repeated is `joined`, a thing and its own part is `related`, two items
   of a group are `regular`, two regions are `section`.

When none fits, the layout is asking for a new role: say so, don't pick the
nearest number.

## Writing them

- **As a class:** any spacing or offset utility takes the role's name:
  `gap-related`, `p-window`, `px-control-x`, `ml-cursor-column`,
  `right-regular`.
- **In a calc:** `--theme(--spacing-cursor)`, for example
  `right-[calc(100%+--theme(--spacing-cursor))]`. Not `var(--spacing-…)`:
  that takes the page root's `--bui-px` instead of the stage's.
- **In code:** `SPACING` and `SpacingRole` from `@sebald/blip-ui`, with
  `px(SPACING.regular, FLOOR)` for an inline style.
- A `className` passed to a component replaces its role as any Tailwind
  class would (`p-4` replaces `p-window`).
- There is no role called `screen`: `--spacing-screen` would take over
  Tailwind's `h-screen` and `w-screen`.

## What stays outside

- **Art on the grid** (a window's frame, a tab's rings, bitmap letters)
  counts in `--px`, the stage's own pixel, not in spacing roles.
- **Set against the letters:** a speaker's mark and a ledger's dots sit on
  the text in `em` (`mt-[0.4em]`, `mb-[0.3em]`).
- **Touch parts** are sized for a thumb in CSS pixels: the tall stage's bar
  of controls and its icon buttons.

## Checked by tests

`foundations/uniformity.test.ts` fails on any padding, margin, gap or
offset in a component written as a number (`calc(var(--bui-px)*4)`,
`(--bui-px)`, `p-6`) instead of a role. It also fails on a part's own length set from one
(`[--b-inline:calc(var(--bui-px)*6)]`). The few exceptions above are listed
in it, each with its reason. The cursor's room is measured on every story
by the visual tests (`cursorRoom`).
