# Duel

A screen where two sides trade lines against a clock: what was said, the
other side's bubble, how the exchange stands, and a log. Everything the
player reads is on the one screen.

## When to use

- A turn-based exchange with a time limit and a score on each side, as in
  an argument, a negotiation or a quiz.
- The player needs the state at a glance (the pools) and the history one tap
  away (the log), without leaving the screen.

## Parts

| Part                 | What it does here                                                                |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SpeechBubble`       | What the other side just said, tail down to them, in a corner at the top         |
| `Timer`              | The clock for the answer, in the opposite top corner                             |
| `Window` and `Line`  | The exchange: the line being answered is `past`, the reply is live               |
| `Tabs`               | Two panels beside the exchange, the pools and the log                            |
| `Ledger` and `Meter` | The pools: a label and a meter per side, the other side in `variant="secondary"` |

## Layout

Everything is placed on the stage in stage pixels. The exchange and the tabs
sit side by side on the wide stage (3 to 2) and stack on the tall one, where
the bottom edge also clears the bar of controls (`--bui-bar`), which the
`Stage` sets. Put the screen on a `Stage` with its `Controls`.
