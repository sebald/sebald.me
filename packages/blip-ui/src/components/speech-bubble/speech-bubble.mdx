# SpeechBubble

A comic speech bubble: paper with ink letters and a tail that points to
whoever is speaking. Use it for a line a character says on the stage, over
a scene. Text the screen itself says (menus, results) belongs in a Window.

## Anatomy

1. **Ink**, the outline, with stepped corners (`[2, 1]`)
2. **Paper**, one step in, with a row of dithered shade along the bottom
3. **Tail**, ink rows widening from the tip with the paper inside them. It
   reaches 5 stage pixels (`TAIL_REACH`) past the edge
4. **Text**, in the paper's foreground colour

## Tail

`side` puts the tail on the `top` (pointing up to a speaker above) or the
`bottom`. `align` measures `offset`, in stage pixels, from the `end` (right)
or `start` (left) edge to the tail's centre.

## Usage

- **Do** aim the tail at the speaker and leave room for it past the edge.
- **Do** keep a bubble to a line or two.
- **Don't** put controls inside a bubble. It is a line of speech.

## Accessibility

- A `<p>`: the text is read as any paragraph. The outline, the tail and the
  shade are `aria-hidden`
- Text is `--bui-paper-foreground` on `--bui-paper`; keep the pair readable
  when you theme them
