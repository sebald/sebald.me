# Controls

The touch versions of a game's keys. On the tall stage (a phone held
upright) a bar along the bottom, `--bui-bar` tall; on the wide stage small
pixel buttons in the corners. It knows no buttons of its own: each button
says what it is with its `slot`, and Controls places it, so the same JSX
works in both layouts. The buttons take their look from it, so they have
no layout prop.

## Anatomy

1. **`slot="leave"`**, what leaves the screen (Back, Pause): the top left
   corner, or the left of the bar
2. **`slot="system"`**, the game's switches (the sound, fullscreen), the
   default: the top right corner, or the right of the bar, where the labels
   come first and the icons sit at the edge
3. **Bar**, along the bottom of the tall stage, with a line above it

## Layouts

| Layout | Where                                              | Shown                           |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Wide   | Corners, 5 stage pixels in                         | For touch (`show="touch"`)      |
| Tall   | A bar of `--bui-bar` along the bottom of the stage | Always, it is the touch control |

On the tall stage the `Stage` writes `--bui-bar` (its `bar` prop, 52 CSS
pixels), so screens keep clear of it: pad by `var(--bui-bar)`. Elsewhere it
is 0.

## Usage

- **Do** give each button its `slot`, `leave` for what leaves the screen;
  the rest are `system`. Where they go is Controls' rule, not the screen's.
- **Do** give each button a `label`: the bar shows it, a corner shows the
  icon alone.
- **Do** keep the keys in the game. Controls has none: bind Esc and M with
  `useKeys` and have the buttons call the same actions.
- **Don't** put more than two or three buttons in a slot.

```tsx
useKeys({ Escape: back, m: toggleSound });

<Controls>
  <IconButton
    slot="leave"
    icon="back"
    label="Back"
    aria-label="Back (Esc)"
    onClick={back}
  />
  <IconToggle
    icon="soundOff"
    pressedIcon="soundOn"
    label="Sound off"
    pressedLabel="Sound on"
    aria-label="Sound (M)"
    pressed={sound}
    onPressedChange={toggleSound}
  />
</Controls>;
```

## Input

Corners show for touch only: `useInputKind` follows the last input, so a
keyboard player never sees them. The tall bar is always there.

## Accessibility

- Each button is a native `<button>` with an `aria-label`
- Tab order follows the DOM: start, then end
