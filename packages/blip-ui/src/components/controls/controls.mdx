# Controls

The touch versions of a game's keys. On the tall stage (a phone held
upright) a bar along the bottom, `--bui-bar` tall; on the wide stage small
pixel buttons in the corners. It knows no buttons of its own: you put them
in two `ControlGroup`s, and the same JSX works in both layouts. The buttons
take their look from it, so they have no layout prop.

## Anatomy

1. **ControlGroup `side="start"`**, the top left corner, or the left of the
   bar: Back or Pause
2. **ControlGroup `side="end"`**, the top right corner, or the right of the
   bar: sound, fullscreen; on the bar the labels come first, the icons at
   the edge
3. **Bar**, along the bottom of the tall stage, with a line above it

## Layouts

| Layout | Where                                              | Shown                           |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Wide   | Corners, 5 stage pixels in                         | For touch (`show="touch"`)      |
| Tall   | A bar of `--bui-bar` along the bottom of the stage | Always, it is the touch control |

On the tall stage the `Stage` writes `--bui-bar` (its `bar` prop, 52 CSS
pixels), so screens keep clear of it: pad by `var(--bui-bar)`. Elsewhere it
is 0.

## Usage

- **Do** put `IconButton`s and `IconToggle`s in the groups, with a
  `label`: the bar shows it, a corner shows the icon alone.
- **Do** keep the keys in the game. Controls has none: bind Esc and M with
  `useKeys` and have the buttons call the same actions.
- **Don't** put more than two or three buttons in a group.

```tsx
useKeys({ Escape: back, m: toggleSound });

<Controls>
  <ControlGroup side="start">
    <IconButton
      icon="back"
      label="Back"
      aria-label="Back (Esc)"
      onClick={back}
    />
  </ControlGroup>
  <ControlGroup side="end">
    <IconToggle
      icon="soundOff"
      pressedIcon="soundOn"
      label="Sound off"
      pressedLabel="Sound on"
      aria-label="Sound (M)"
      pressed={sound}
      onPressedChange={toggleSound}
    />
  </ControlGroup>
</Controls>;
```

## Input

Corners show for touch only: `useInputKind` follows the last input, so a
keyboard player never sees them. The tall bar is always there.

## Accessibility

- Each button is a native `<button>` with an `aria-label`
- Tab order follows the DOM: start, then end
