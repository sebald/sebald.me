# Switch

A toggle switch, the checkbox of a menu: one option that is on or off
(Music, Hints, Screen shake). It
shows its name, a track with a square at one end, and the state in words
(On, Off), and a tap, Left, Right, Enter or Space flips it. Built on Base
UI's Switch (the switch role, `aria-checked`, the form value).

<Controls of={SwitchStories.Default} />

```tsx
<Switch checked={music} onCheckedChange={setMusic}>
  Music
</Switch>
```

## A toggle of its own, not a Setting

`Setting` shows a value between two arrows and has no keys of its own; in a
Picker, `PickerSetting` takes the arrows. A toggle switch is different behaviour:
it is a control you can focus (the `switch` role with `aria-checked`), it
flips on Enter, Space and a tap as well as the arrows, and it holds a form
value. That is semantics and keys no `Setting` variant has, and it is the
primitive Base UI already provides, which a `Setting` could not wrap. So it
is a component of its own; a menu row of two values that you step through
stays a `PickerSetting`.

## Anatomy

1. **Name**, the children: the option, which is also what a screen reader
   reads
2. **Track**, a slot with a frame that lights when on
3. **Thumb**, a square that moves from one end to the other
4. **State**, On or Off, in words, so the state never rests on colour

## Usage

Every toggle works the same: the state is data on the root (`data-checked`), which the styles read, so a
switch controlled by the caller (`checked`) and one that keeps its own
(`defaultChecked`) look the same. The thumb moves with a short transition
and snaps under reduced motion. A change plays the `move` sound through
`useFeedback()`, like a value stepping.

## Input

- A tap anywhere on the row, Enter and Space flip it
- Left and Right flip it too, as the arrows step a two-value Setting; the
  screen's own keys do not also hear them while it has the focus
- A disabled switch ignores all of them

## Accessibility

- `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`, named by its children; the On and Off
  words are hidden from assistive technology as `aria-checked` says the
  same
- Takes the focus and shows the kit's focus ring from the keyboard
- A touch screen gets a row at least 44 CSS pixels high
