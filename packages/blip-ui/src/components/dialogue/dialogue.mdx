# Dialogue

One spoken line with a small square in front of it that says who speaks.
Use it for a back-and-forth where the reader must tell the sides apart
without names.

<Canvas of={DialogueStories.Default} />
<Controls of={DialogueStories.Default} />

## Anatomy

1. **Mark**, a small square in the speaker's colour
2. **Line**, the words; a gap of 3 `--bui-px` between them

## Variants

| Tone        | Mark  | Who                           |
| ----------- | ----- | ----------------------------- |
| `primary`   | Gold  | The player, the first speaker |
| `secondary` | Ember | The opponent, the other side  |

`muted` fades a line that is over: the phrase being answered, a reply
already shown.

<Canvas of={DialogueStories.Overview} />

## Usage

- **Do** use the same tone for the same speaker throughout.
- **Do** mute the line being answered, so the live one stands out.

<Canvas of={DialogueStories.Exchange} />

- **Don't** rely on the mark's colour alone to name a speaker where it
  matters. Say it in the words or the surrounding text.

## Accessibility

- A `<p>`; the mark is `aria-hidden` and carries no meaning for assistive
  technology
- The muted colour is a quieter text colour (`--bui-muted`), not a lower
  opacity
