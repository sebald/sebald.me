Spacing by role instead of by number: `gap-related`, `p-window`, `ml-cursor-column` and the rest (`foundations/spacing.mdx`). Layout uses 1, 2, 4 and 8 stage pixels, the cursor 3, 6 and 12. `SPACE` is replaced by `SPACING` and `SpacingRole`. A few spaces move to their role: window titles, Switch and Readout labels, the screen and dialog edges, the speech bubble's top padding, and button padding.
