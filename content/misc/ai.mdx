## Writing

The notes are mine. The topics, the arguments and the experiences behind them come from my work. I write most of the first drafts on my commute.

Then I edit with Claude. It gives me the kind of feedback a good editor would, whenever I have a few minutes for it: it points out sentences that are vague, arguments that don't hold, and paragraphs that say the same thing twice. Sometimes it suggests a better wording, and sometimes I take it. I decide what stays, and nothing gets published that I haven't read and would not say myself.

## Images

I love the pixel art of old adventure games, but I can't draw it. So the headers start as images generated with Gemini. I snap them to their real pixel grid and animate a few small details by hand. The prompt and the process are [in the repository](https://github.com/sebald/sebald.me/tree/main/.claude/skills/pixel-header).

## Code

I designed this site and wrote its first version by hand. Today I build it together with Claude Code: I decide how things should look and feel, and Claude helps me turn that into code. That leaves me more time for what the site is actually for: writing, rather than working on version 4-final-final-2 of the site. You can browse the building blocks in the [inventory](/inventory), and all of the code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/sebald/sebald.me).
